About Musafir

A little place. A lot of stories.

Musafir means traveller. It is a small corner by the road with some books, a warm cup of karak or a chilled drink, and space to just be for a while.

Come with family, with friends, or on your own. Sit, talk about your ideas, meet someone new, or do nothing at all and enjoy the evening.

Our story

Something was missing in Attock. So they built it.

Musafir was built by hand, one piece at a time, and shared on Instagram as it happened. Here is the journey in the order it was posted.

Acha, ab yeh bhi karna hai “Okay, now this needs doing too.” The phrase of the build.

  1. An idea for Attock

    A place for late-night cravings, spontaneous plans and good conversations, with tea, coffee, fresh juices and ice cream on the way.

  2. A handmade signboard

    Bamboo poles and wooden planks from Gondal Bazaar in Hazro, a dark brown finish, and lettering painted by hand. No printing, no machines.

  3. From a small empty room

    One wall, one window, one shelf and one coat of paint at a time, through unexpected costs, changes and late nights.

  4. A gift from Asra

    A handmade crochet sunflower, a keychain and a handwritten note arrived while the space was still being built.

  5. Learning in Lahore

    A trip to learn shakes, iced teas, mojitos and mocktails, experiment with flavours and bring the best ideas home.

  6. Soft launch

    A sunset, good drinks, families and the first guests. Open daily from 5 PM to 12 AM.

  7. Twenty days in

    UNO with families, painting, a first workshop and the first Musafir Jamming Night. Still being built, one evening at a time.
